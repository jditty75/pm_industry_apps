VOICE OF CUSTOMER

About the Survey Forecast Explorer

Purpose, scheduling logic and how to use VOC

Purpose

The VOC Survey Forecast Explorer provides a forward-looking, month-by-month view of expected Mid-Deployment Surveys (MDS) and Post Go-Live Surveys (PGL), identifying the customers, deployments and products associated with each expected distribution.

How it works

VOC uses Salesforce-derived deployment, product-function and contact data for State and Local Government (SLG), Higher Education and Nonprofit (HENP), and Healthcare (HC). Active deployments remain eligible for Mid-Deployment Survey (MDS) and Post Go-Live Survey (PGL) forecasting. Complete deployments may still appear when a projected PGL distribution falls in the selected forecast month. Workday Professional Services deployments appear by default; the partner checkbox adds partner deployments.

Completed Deployments and PGL Forecasts

A deployment's Salesforce completion status does not determine whether its projected PGL forecast remains relevant for survey planning. Complete deployments are included only in forecast months that contain their projected PGL event; they do not generate MDS forecasts. PGL timing uses each product function's actual production date when recorded, otherwise its target date—the same product-function authority as Active deployments. Records receive an informational Completed Deployment — PGL Forecast Active indicator (not a data-quality defect). Historical, current, and future forecast months use the same inclusion rules. These projections do not establish actual Qualtrics survey activity.

Example: A deployment completed in September may still have a projected PGL distribution in November. VoC retains that projection even though the deployment is no longer Active.
MDS · Mid-Deployment
  • Starts with the deployment start date and each product's target Move to Production (MTP) date.
  • Calculates the one-third milestone of that timeline.
  • Forecasts the first Wednesday of the following month.
  • Groups product milestones that fall in the same distribution month into one MDS distribution per deployment.
PGL · Post Go-Live
  • Uses each product's actual MTP date where available.
  • Forecasts the first Wednesday two calendar months after the go-live month.
  • When actual MTP is missing, uses target MTP as a predicted PGL date.
  • Groups milestones falling in the same distribution month into one PGL distribution per deployment.

Product-function dates are authoritative

VOC analyzes product-function delivery cohorts—groups of product functions that share the same target Move to Production (MTP) date—not the parent deployment record alone.

  • Deployment Start Date — common start for duration and MDS calculations.
  • Product-function target MTP — planned cohort milestone (Production_Move_Date_Target__c).
  • Product-function actual MTP — recorded go-live when available (Production_Move_Date_Actual__c).
  • Parent Current / First Actual MTP — supporting context and reconciliation; they never override valid product-function dates.

Delivery structure classifications

Big Bang
  • Every product function shares one valid target MTP.
  • Parent target MTP matches that date.
  • One planned delivery cohort; parent and product dates corroborate.
Multi-Deliverable
  • Multiple distinct product-function target MTP dates.
  • Each date is its own planned cohort.
  • Parent MTP may match only one cohort.
Single Product Cohort
  • All dated product functions share one target MTP.
  • Parent target may be blank or disagree; product dates still govern.
Insufficient Data
  • Missing start or product milestones prevent reliable cohort duration or forecast.
  • VOC does not invent or substitute parent dates for product milestones.

Deployment Duration

  • Planned cohort duration — Deployment Start → product-function target MTP (whole UTC calendar days).
  • Actual duration — Deployment Start → product-function actual MTP when recorded; never assumed from parent actual MTP alone.
  • Single / Big Bang cohort — One duration line (actual when every function in the cohort shares the same actual date; otherwise planned).
  • Multi-Deliverable — Range of planned cohort durations, e.g. 18.0–32.0 weeks · 3 cohorts; sort uses the longest planned cohort.
  • Display — Weeks (one decimal), days, approximate months (÷ 30.4375, ~). Unavailable when no valid span exists.

MDS duration qualification (>16 weeks)

Evaluated per planned delivery cohort. MDS requires duration strictly greater than 112 calendar days (16 weeks): 111 and 112 days do not qualify; 113 days qualifies. This does not remove MDS projections—it flags qualification only. PGL has no 16-week rule.

MDS & PGL forecast rules (unchanged formulas)

  • MDS — Per target-date cohort: one-third milestone between start and target MTP → first Wednesday of the following month. Same-month cohorts still group into one monthly distribution.
  • PGL — Per product function: actual MTP when present, else target → first Wednesday two calendar months after go-live month.

Duration filter & indicators

Hide deployments ≤16 weeks (off by default) hides a deployment only when every planned cohort has a valid duration at or below 112 days. Deployments with any longer cohort, unknown duration, or partial data stay visible unless all evaluable cohorts are short.

The ≤16 weeks pill shows one short cohort, or the count of short cohorts when several exist.

Survey Opportunity Insights

VoC uses existing Salesforce deployment and product-function dates to highlight noteworthy survey-planning circumstances. These are informational observations only—they do not modify MDS/PGL eligibility or forecasting, do not affect distribution inclusion, are not warnings or risk indicators, and do not establish whether Qualtrics has scheduled or distributed a survey. Review supporting dates and product functions in Survey Intelligence.

Additional Product Go-Live
  • Identifies a PGL projection tied to a recorded product-function actual production date later than the parent deployment's first actual production milestone.
  • Uses actual product-function dates only—not target dates.
Completed Product Deployment
  • Identifies a Complete deployment with a focused product delivery cohort (Big Bang or Single Product Cohort) and a projected PGL distribution in the selected forecast month.
  • Classification uses product-function cohort evidence, not deployment naming.

Survey Intelligence

The Survey Intelligence drawer tab consolidates executive outlook, MDS/PGL planning detail, and the expandable survey timeline. Observations are generated by deterministic Apps Script rules from Salesforce evidence—no AI or Qualtrics execution data.

Labels & indicators

These labels explain how to interpret the forecast. They describe planning information, not proof that a survey invitation has been sent. The Review column shows at most one compact indicator per deployment; open Survey Intelligence for full explanations.

Complete · PGL only (beneath the deployment name) identifies a Complete deployment with a projected PGL distribution in the selected month. That status is not repeated in the Review column. Survey Opportunity Insights are informational only. None of these labels change forecast dates, survey eligibility, or distribution inclusion. Actual survey administration remains managed independently through Qualtrics.

Workday PS

Deployment partner is Workday Professional Services. These deployments appear by default. Turn on Include partner deployments to see others as well.

Complete · PGL only

The deployment is Complete in Salesforce but has a projected PGL distribution in the selected forecast period. Shown beneath the deployment name for survey planning—not in the Review column and not a Salesforce data-quality warning.

Review

Indicates an existing forecast or data-quality consideration that may warrant review. Potentially inconsistent product-level dates need a closer look; VOC preserves source values and projections.

Survey Insight

An informational observation identifying a potentially noteworthy survey-planning opportunity (for example, an additional product go-live or a completed focused product deployment).

Review + Insight

Both an existing review consideration and a Survey Opportunity Insight apply. Open Survey Intelligence for each explanation.

MDS

Mid-Deployment Survey. The distribution month is based on the one-third milestone between deployment start and product target MTP.

PGL

Post Go-Live Survey. The distribution month is based on an actual MTP date when available, or a target date for a prediction.

PGL · Predicted

No actual MTP date is recorded for the contributing product date group. VOC estimates the PGL distribution from its target MTP date; the month may change.

PGL · Mixed

One grouped monthly PGL distribution contains both actual-date-based and target-date-based predicted product calculations.

Actual-based

The PGL timing uses a recorded actual MTP date. This does not confirm that the survey was sent.

2 product-date groups

Multiple distinct product milestone dates roll up into one survey distribution for the same deployment, survey type and month. Expand it to see the products and calculations.

≤16 weeks

At least one planned cohort is at or below 112 days (16 weeks) for MDS duration qualification. Does not suppress MDS projections.

Using the explorer

Select a month, choose an industry and survey type, and review the deployment rows. Open Survey Intelligence for cohort-level MDS/PGL detail and projected distribution timing.

Forecast only. Actual survey administration is managed in Qualtrics. VOC projects survey timing from Salesforce-derived dates. It does not send surveys, track Qualtrics completion, or label forecasts as missed, sent, or overdue.
