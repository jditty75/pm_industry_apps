# LLM guardrails (documentation only)

Companion to the concise instruction in [ai-reasoning-contract.md](./ai-reasoning-contract.md). Apply for Deployment Signals reasoning — not production **HENP Exec Summary_GAS**.

- Use only supplied evidence; distinguish fact from interpretation.
- No causal inference from sequence or correlation alone.
- No partner blame without explicit evidence in sources.
- No predictions that a deployment will fail, escalate, or miss production.
- No manufactured reasons for health or schedule movement.
- Prioritize compound independent indicators over single-field noise.
- Include meaningful recovery and positive trajectories.
- Do not generate signals merely to fill categories.
- Preserve existing past-MTP exclusion for Active deployments where Exec Summary rules require it.
- No cross-industry comparison in industry-specific output.
- Favor leadership questions and considerations over unsupported prescriptions.
